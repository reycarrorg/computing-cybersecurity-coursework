# IS 3003 — Principles of Information Systems for Management

The University of Texas at San Antonio · Spring 2026 · business information systems

My written work focused on how information systems affect privacy and organizational decisions. I examined why a person's willingness to share information changes with the purpose and context of a request, and why combining otherwise public records in people-search services can create a new privacy risk.

The practical takeaway I carry forward is to ask what data an organization truly needs, how it will be combined, who can access it, and whether people would reasonably expect that use. These are questions about governance and trust, not just technical access controls.

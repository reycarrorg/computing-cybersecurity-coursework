# IS 3413 — Telecommunications and Networking

The University of Texas at San Antonio · Fall 2025 · network fundamentals

## Packet Tracer: seeing a small network as a system

In a course-provided Cisco Packet Tracer activity, I assembled a small office-style physical layout, connected wired and wireless devices, explored device configuration, and used a network-controller view to understand what was active. The controller made the topology easier to reason about than a collection of isolated device screens. My original report also says that I needed to revisit instructions when the sequence became confusing; it is evidence of an introductory lab, not independent network-deployment experience.

## Wireshark: comparing transport behavior

Using a course-provided practice capture, I inspected DNS traffic carried over UDP and a TCP connection with its SYN, SYN-ACK, and ACK exchange. I compared the smaller UDP header with TCP's sequence, acknowledgment, and control fields, and observed HTTP within a TCP stream. The exercise helped me connect protocol concepts to visible packet fields. It did not involve capturing or publishing traffic from a live third-party network.
